Appearance

Themes

Eight colour themes, from pure black for OLED screens to a light theme.

What it is

StarkLLM has eight themes: Midnight Indigo (default), OLED Pitch Black, Liquid Glass, Cyberpunk Rose, Matrix Emerald, Sunset Amber, Arctic Frost and Daylight Minimal.

Why it matters

Long sessions are easier on the eyes when contrast and brightness fit your screen and your room.

How StarkLLM does it

Change the theme with the palette button at the bottom of the sidebar or in Settings, Appearance. The choice is saved and applied immediately.

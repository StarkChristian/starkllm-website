Product

How StarkLLM works

A local pipeline built for privacy: it reads your documents and answers with sources on your own PC. Since 2.5.0 it also does tasks for you, on the web, in a project folder and from your phone.

New in 2.5.0

Tasks it does for you

StarkLLM can now act, not only answer. It shows what it is about to do and waits for your yes at every step that matters.

Web tasks

  • Its own browser windowStart a "Web task" in the chat. StarkLLM opens a separate browser window and you watch every step. "Take over" lets you work in the window yourself and hand it back.
  • It asks firstBefore anything that buys, books, pays, sends or deletes, StarkLLM stops and asks. Bank, health and government sites are opened only after your yes, adult sites never.
  • Some steps are always yoursSign-ins, payments and "are you a robot" checks are never done by StarkLLM.
  • Again tomorrowSave the page to the workspace, keep the task for later or repeat it daily or weekly. A repeated task only looks, and a notification tells you when the answer changed.

Terminal: help with code

  • One project folderChoose a folder with "Terminal". StarkLLM reads and changes files there and runs PowerShell commands. It never leaves that folder and never reads keys or .env files.
  • Your yes for every stepEvery change is shown first, and every command that can change something runs only after your yes. Commands that change Windows or its security are refused.
  • Undo"Undo the file changes" puts the files back as they were.

Web tasks and the terminal work best with a local model of 14B or more (for example qwen3:14b); small 7B models stop safely but finish few tasks.

Also new

More ways to work with your files

  • Questions about an image

    Paste a screenshot or choose a photo, and a vision model on your PC answers. The image is made smaller, its hidden data (place, camera) is removed, and it is not stored.

  • Recordings become documents

    Meetings, lectures and voice memos are written down on your PC with times, by Whisper large-v3-turbo (downloaded once, about 1.6 GB). Then you ask about them like about any document.

  • Two models side by side

    "Compare models" asks a second local model the same question. You keep the better answer.

  • Word and PDF, with sources

    Save a whole chat or a single answer as a Word document or a PDF, with the sources it is based on.

  • Your own tool servers (MCP)

    Add MCP servers that run on your PC in Settings › Local Tools. Every tool is off until you turn it on, and every call waits for your yes.

  • Web search with fallback engines

    When you turn on web search, your question goes to DuckDuckGo. Only if it does not answer, StarkLLM tries Mullvad Leta, then Mojeek: one engine at a time, never several at once.

Android · preview

Your phone joins in

The StarkLLM app for Android works together with your PC. The documents and the AI model stay on the PC; the phone asks, sends photos of letters and shows your deadlines. There is no iPhone app; the Android app is a preview and works on your home network only.

  • Paired by QR codeSettings › Phone shows a QR code. A phone reaches your PC only after it was paired, and it stays paired until you remove it, or until its account signs out or changes its password.
  • Home network only, encryptedThe phone connects over your home network only. The connection is encrypted, and the phone accepts only your PC's own certificate, fixed at pairing, so no other device in the Wi-Fi can pose as your PC.
  • Never online from the phoneA question from the phone stays on your PC: no web search and no cloud models. "Fully offline" on the PC closes the link.
  • Several accounts on one PCEach phone belongs to the StarkLLM account that showed its QR code.

Per phone, you choose what it may do

  1. Ask onlyReads workspaces, chats, the inbox and deadlines, and asks questions. It does not send documents or change deadlines.
  2. Ask and send documentsAlso sends photos and documents, deletes chats and marks deadlines as done.
  3. Ask, send and manage this PCAlso starts a backup and can switch StarkLLM fully offline. The phone asks for your fingerprint first.

The Android app is a preview in a closed test on Google Play. Using it away from your home network is not part of this version.

The document lifecycle

When you add a PDF or text file, processing stays on your PC.

  1. ParseLocal parsers extract the raw text. Scanned PDFs and images go through local OCR or vision before chunking.
  2. ChunkA semantic chunking algorithm splits the text into logical, overlapping blocks.
  3. EmbedA local embedding model turns each block into a vector, stored in your private ChromaDB database on your PC.
  4. AnswerWhen you ask a question, the most relevant passages are injected into the context window of your chosen Ollama model, so the response is grounded in your sources.

This is the chat. Ask about your documents, and the answer shows where it comes from.

Document inbox

Every letter, read and filed

Add a letter as a PDF, a scan or a photo from your phone. StarkLLM reads it on your PC and suggests what you need. It never renames, moves or deletes your files. Only Terminal changes files, and only in a project folder you choose, after showing each change; "Undo the file changes" puts them back.

  • What it readsThe kind of letter (bill, reminder, tax notice, contract, fine and more), sender, letter date, reference numbers such as customer, contract or tax number, amounts, bank accounts and deadlines, in letters in English, German, French, Spanish, Italian and Turkish.
  • Every value has a sourceEach value is found word for word on a page. Click it and the letter opens at that page with the line marked.
  • Cases per senderLetters of one sender are put together, the case with the nearest deadline first. The same letter as a photo and as a PDF is marked, and so are a replaced letter and a possible scam.
  • You decideMark a letter done or give it your own title. The number of new letters is shown next to "Inbox".
Deadlines

Deadlines on the start page

The start page shows your newest documents and the deadlines, appointments and payments found in them.

  • With amount and pageEvery date comes with its sentence, the amount if there is one and the page it was read from.
  • Add to calendar"Add to calendar" saves the dates as a calendar file on request.
  • Made by rules on this PCDates, amounts and senders are found by fixed rules over the text of your letters, not guessed by a model, so each one is in the document. Check the letter before you act on it.
  • LanguagesLetters in English, German, French, Spanish, Italian and Turkish are understood, with their deadlines and amounts. Photos and scans are read as English or German text; more text-recognition languages can be added in Settings. Letters in other languages are filed and can be searched, but without deadlines and amounts. The interface speaks the same six languages.
Verifiable intelligence

Answers you can check

Language models can produce unsupported claims when they answer from memory alone. StarkLLM addresses this with Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).

Source-backed responses

The AI retrieves text passages from your exact documents before it formulates an answer.

Passages you can read

Responses cite the source filename and a page or section locator. Click a source under an answer to see the passages it was based on, word for word, and open the document at that page.

Warnings for fake letters

A red box above the answer appears if a letter asks for payment to a foreign account, by voucher or through a link, contains text addressed to AI assistants, or a sender suddenly names a different account.

Persistent library

A knowledge base that stays in sync

Workspace uploads suit isolated projects. The Global Knowledge Base is your permanent reference library, reachable from every chat.

  • Local folder indexingMap any folder on your Windows machine. StarkLLM recursively indexes supported files into a global vector collection.
  • Universal accessQuery the Knowledge Base from any chat, whatever workspace you are in.
  • Interval synchronizationEvery 30 seconds, the Knowledge Base checks the mapped folder and re-indexes new or modified files.

Workspace documents or Knowledge Base?

StarkLLM offers two ways to give the AI context. Knowing the difference keeps your material organized.

Workspace documents

  • Tied to one single workspace.
  • Useful when work must stay isolated to prevent context contamination.
  • Best for temporary or project-specific document sets.

ExampleA folder of contracts for one legal case or client audit.

Knowledge Base

  • A persistent, global layer.
  • Useful across all chats and workspaces at once.
  • Best for long-term reference material and personal archives.

ExampleYour ongoing research library, handbook or company SOPs.

Local control

Tune it to your hardware

StarkLLM gives you administrative control over your local inference environment.

  • Model selectionSwitch between any model you downloaded via Ollama (for example Qwen 2.5, Llama 3.2, Mistral) from the chat interface.
  • Runtime configurationAdjust context window size, temperature and advanced parameters to balance speed and reasoning depth.
  • Strict localhost routingInternal API traffic runs only on localhost ports of your PC (127.0.0.1), keeping your data on your machine. Only when you turn on Settings › Phone can a paired phone on your home network reach the app.
In-process local MCP host

Tools that ask before they act

Unlike cloud AI tools that execute commands remotely, StarkLLM runs a tool host inside its own FastAPI backend. Models get verified, read-only access to your indexed content.

  • Three built-in toolslist_workspace_files inspects active workspace files. search_local_kb runs hybrid vector and BM25 search over personal archives. fetch_cited_chunk fetches the exact passage text.
  • Human-in-the-loop gateEach tool call creates a card in the chat. Review the parameters, change values, or cancel.
  • Adversarial prompt defenseRetrieved passages are stripped of tool-call markers before they reach the model context, which makes prompt injection through documents harder (no technique removes that risk completely).
  • Server-side execution gateA single-use invocation token blocks direct execution and replay attempts.
in_process_tool: list_workspace_files

Why it differs from basic local chat clients

Many desktop clients offer a chat wrapper or naive document chunking. StarkLLM is built as a precision workstation.

Hybrid RAG with a cross-encoder

Most desktop RAG tools rely on vector embeddings alone and can miss exact keywords. StarkLLM merges BM25 keyword frequency with dense vector search, then re-ranks chunks with a local TinyBERT cross-encoder.

Native standalone window

No browser tabs or address bar. A standalone desktop app with a frameless title bar, splash screen, system tray integration and a Windows NSIS installer.

Python code runner

Run Python calculations, data analysis and formula work on your own machine, with code checks, memory caps and time limits. Available with the Docker Desktop engine (advanced); off in the built-in engine until StarkLLM has its own sandbox. These are convenience limits, not a security boundary: only run code you trust.

Local OpenAI-compatible API

A local gateway exposing /v1/chat/completions and /v1/models. Connect IDEs like Cursor, tools like Claude Code, Obsidian plugins or your own Python scripts to your local models.

Your documents, answered in private.

In briefA Windows app that reads your files on your own computer and cites the page it used. No account, no upload.
Download v2.0.0

Most assistants send what you ask, and what you attach, to someone else's server. StarkLLM keeps the whole loop on the machine in front of you.

Runs on Ollama and Docker Desktop. Every answer links back to its source passage.

What is in the box

Retrieval
Hybrid RAG with page and section locators, so each citation opens the right place in the PDF.
Local tools
Read-only tools such as list_workspace_files and fetch_cited_chunk. You approve every call.
Interface
Native desktop window, chat folders, eight themes, export to Markdown, JSON, HTML and text.
Hardware
8 GB RAM runs 3B models. 16 GB RAM and an 8 GB GPU suit 7B models.
Licence
Apache 2.0. Free and open source.
If it cannot be uploaded, it should still be searchable.

Privacy Policy

Data protection under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)

Courtesy translation. The German version is legally binding. This English text is provided for information only. Read the German original here: Datenschutzerklärung.

1. Data protection at a glance

General information: The protection of your personal data is important to us. StarkLLM is designed as a private, local open-source research project. By default, the application itself runs locally on your own hardware. This statement informs you about the nature, scope and purpose of the collection and use of personal data on this website.

3. Data collection on our website

Hosting and server log files

When you visit our website, the browser on your device automatically sends information to the server of our website. This information is stored temporarily in a so-called log file. The following data is collected without any action on your part:

  • IP address of the requesting computer
  • Date and time of the access
  • Name and URL of the file requested
  • Website from which the access is made (referrer URL)
  • The browser used and, where applicable, the operating system of your computer

The legal basis for the data processing is Art. 6(1) sentence 1 lit. f GDPR. Our legitimate interest follows from the purposes of ensuring a smooth connection to the website and ensuring system security and stability.

Fonts (local system fonts)

This website does not embed external fonts (such as Google Fonts) from third-party servers. Only the system's own standard fonts are used, so no data is transmitted to third parties to display fonts.

Privacy-friendly web analytics (Umami)

This website uses the privacy-friendly open-source analytics software Umami (self-hosted). Umami does not set cookies, does not store personal data and does not track users across different websites. All IP addresses are processed in anonymised form. The collection serves exclusively to statistically evaluate use in order to improve our website (Art. 6(1) lit. f GDPR).

Contact by email

If you contact us by email, your email, including the contact details you provide, is stored by us for the purpose of processing your request and in case of follow-up questions. We do not pass this data on without your consent. The legal basis is Art. 6(1) lit. b or lit. f GDPR.

4. Local StarkLLM software (desktop app)

The StarkLLM Windows application works on the local-first principle:

  • All documents, vector databases (ChromaDB) and user accounts are stored exclusively locally on your computer.
  • The language models run locally on your CPU/GPU through Ollama.
  • The software does not transmit any telemetry or usage data to external servers.

The following functions connect to the internet only if you switch them on or trigger them yourself:

  • Web search (switch in the chat): your question is sent to DuckDuckGo.
  • Cloud model providers (settings, e.g. DeepSeek, OpenRouter, Groq): your question and the document passages found are sent to the provider you have chosen.
  • YouTube and GitHub import: the app downloads the video transcript or repository you specify.
  • Market prices (widget, off by default): retrieved from Yahoo Finance.
  • Update check (asked on first start, can be switched off at any time): queries GitHub for the latest version, without usage data.

The privacy policies of the respective provider apply to these connections.

One-time downloads without your data: The AI models you choose (through Ollama), the re-ranking model (TinyBERT, on the first search) and the speech recognition model (Whisper, on the first voice input) are downloaded from the internet once. No documents, questions or usage data are transmitted in the process. Without internet, search works without re-ranking.

5. Your rights

Within the framework of the applicable statutory provisions, you have the right at any time to:

  • information about your personal data stored by us (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • erasure of your data stored by us (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • object to the processing (Art. 21 GDPR)
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)

For this and for further questions on data protection, you can contact us at any time at the address given in the Impressum (legal notice). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR).

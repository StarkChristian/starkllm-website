3. Data collection on our website

Hosting and server log files

This website consists of static pages. It is served by Cloudflare Pages, and every request to starkllm.com passes through Cloudflare's network (provider: Cloudflare, Inc., USA). We keep the source code of the website at GitHub (GitHub, Inc., USA, a subsidiary of Microsoft Corporation); no data is sent there when you view the pages.

When you visit our website, the browser on your device automatically sends information to Cloudflare's servers. The following data is processed and stored by Cloudflare in log files (server logs):

  • IP address of the requesting computer
  • Date and time of the access
  • Name and URL of the file requested
  • Website from which the access is made (referrer URL)
  • The browser used and, where applicable, the operating system of your computer

Cloudflare processes this data to deliver the pages and to protect the website against attacks and abuse. If a connection fails, your browser may also send a technical error report (Network Error Logging) to Cloudflare. Cloudflare hides the email addresses on these pages from spam bots; for this your browser loads a small script from this domain. We do not analyse this data ourselves and do not create usage profiles.

The legal basis for the data processing is Art. 6(1) sentence 1 lit. f GDPR. Our legitimate interest follows from the purposes of ensuring a smooth connection to the website and ensuring system security and stability. Cloudflare may also process data in the USA. The transfer is based on the European Commission's adequacy decision for the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, under which Cloudflare, Inc. is certified. More information: Cloudflare privacy policy.

Downloads from GitHub

The StarkLLM installation files are not stored on this website but on GitHub Releases (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you click a download link or a link to the source code, your browser fetches the file or page directly from GitHub, and GitHub receives your IP address and the technical connection data listed above. GitHub is responsible for this processing: GitHub privacy statement.

Fonts (self-hosted)

This website uses the fonts Geist and Geist Mono. The font files are stored on the server of this website and loaded from there. No connection is made to Google Fonts or any other font provider.

No web analytics, no cookies

This website uses no web analytics and no tracking tools, and it sets no cookies.

Browser storage (Lucy)

The assistant Lucy answers questions entirely in your browser from the pages of this website; your questions are not transmitted. If you hide Lucy, your browser stores this choice in its local storage (localStorage, entry "lucy-hidden"). A conversation with Lucy is kept only in your browser's session storage (sessionStorage) and is deleted when you close the tab. These entries never leave your device and can be deleted in your browser settings. The legal basis is Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG (German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act), because the storage is strictly necessary for the function you asked for.

Contact by email

If you contact us by email, your email, including the contact details you provide, is stored by us for the purpose of processing your request and in case of follow-up questions. We do not pass this data on without your consent. The legal basis is Art. 6(1) lit. b or lit. f GDPR.

4. Local StarkLLM software (desktop app)

The StarkLLM Windows application works on the local-first principle:

  • All documents, vector databases (ChromaDB) and user accounts are stored exclusively locally on your computer.
  • The language models run locally on your CPU/GPU through Ollama.
  • The software does not transmit any telemetry or usage data to external servers.

The following functions connect to the internet only if you switch them on or trigger them yourself:

  • Web search (switch in the chat): your question is sent to DuckDuckGo. Only if DuckDuckGo does not answer is it sent to Startpage, then to Mojeek, and as the last resort to Wikipedia’s public search (Wikimedia Foundation), to one provider at a time.
  • Web tasks (the "Web task" switch in the chat): StarkLLM opens the task's websites in its own window. The operators of those websites receive your IP address and what the task enters there.
  • Cloud model providers (settings, e.g. DeepSeek, OpenRouter, Groq): your question and the document passages found are sent to the provider you have chosen.
  • Ollama cloud models (models whose name ends in ":cloud"): your question and the document passages found are sent through Ollama to Ollama's servers.
  • Import from the web (web page, YouTube, GitHub): the app downloads the web page, video transcript or repository you specify.
  • OCR language packs (Settings › Reading photos and scans): the language file you choose is downloaded from GitHub.
  • Market prices (widget, off by default): retrieved from Yahoo Finance and CoinGecko.
  • Update check (asked on first start, can be switched off at any time): queries GitHub for the latest version, without usage data.

The privacy policies of the respective provider apply to these connections. Settings › Privacy & network lists every connection the app makes, and the "Fully offline" switch refuses all of them. Commands you allow in Terminal and tool servers (MCP) you add yourself run as your own programs; their connections are not in that list.

Phone link on your home network: Only if you turn on the link in Settings › Phone and pair a phone can that paired phone reach the app. At home it connects over your home network; no data is sent to the internet in the process. A phone can be paired at home only.

Phone away from home (StarkLLM relay, off by default): Only if you turn on "Also away from home (through the relay)" for a paired phone in Settings › Phone does the app keep a connection to our relay server relay.starkllm.com, through which this phone can reach the app from other networks (for example mobile data). The relay only passes the connection on: questions, answers, documents and the phone’s access token are encrypted end to end between the phone and your PC (TLS with your PC’s own certificate, which the phone checks), so the relay cannot read them. To pass the connection on, the relay processes connection data: the IP addresses of the phone and the PC, the random ID of your PC, the time and the amount of data. For each PC it stores the random ID, a hash (SHA-256) of a secret of your PC and the date the ID was first used; it stores no content and writes no log of content or IP addresses. The relay is reached through Cloudflare’s network (see section 3). The legal basis is Art. 6(1) lit. b GDPR (providing the function you switched on) and lit. f GDPR (protecting the relay against abuse). Turning the switch off for every phone, or "Fully offline", closes the connection to the relay.

One-time downloads without your data: The AI models you choose (through Ollama), the image model (through Ollama, at the first question about an image), the speech recognition model Whisper base (at the first voice input) and Whisper large-v3-turbo (about 1.6 GB, at the first recording) are downloaded from the internet once. No documents, questions or usage data are transmitted in the process. The search's re-ranking model is part of the app and also works without internet.

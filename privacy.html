Privacy

Private by design. Checkable by you.

StarkLLM is built so that your documents never need a cloud. What goes online is your choice, you can see every connection, and you do not have to take our word for it.

What never leaves your PC

These stay on your own disk and your own processor or graphics card, whatever you switch on.

Your documents

Letters, contracts, books, scans, photos and recordings, and the text read from them.

Your chats

Questions, answers and their sources, unless you choose a cloud model yourself.

The search index

The passages and vectors of every document, and the summaries of large ones.

The AI models

They run on your PC through Ollama. After the first download, StarkLLM works without internet.

Inbox and deadlines

Senders, amounts, accounts and dates are found by rules on your PC.

Nothing about you

No telemetry, no crash reports, no usage statistics, no advertising, no account on a server.

Commands you allow in Terminal and tool servers you add run as your own programs: they are not listed, and what they connect to is up to them. Ollama and Windows have their own update checks, which StarkLLM does not control.

One switch: fully offline

"Fully offline" in Settings › Privacy & network refuses every online feature, closes the link to your phone and the relay, and never downloads anything, not even an update. Your documents, the inbox and the local models keep working. The first start asks once whether to stay online or go fully offline.

StarkLLM

Protection at rest

Optional, in Settings › Protection: for a lost laptop, another user on the same PC, or a backup in a cloud folder.

LimitsIt does not protect against malware that runs as you while StarkLLM is open. BitLocker is still recommended: it also covers the programs and the models.

Encrypted on disk
Documents, chats, names and titles are encrypted on disk, and the search index can be locked too. The key is bound to your Windows account.
Start-up passphrase
An optional passphrase with a recovery key. The passphrase is never stored.
Encrypted backup
One backup file you can keep anywhere, also in a cloud folder; without the passphrase it is noise. An automatic weekly backup keeps several copies.

Signed builds

Since 2.1.2 the installer, StarkLLM.exe, the uninstaller and the program files of the engine are signed with a Certum certificate issued to "Open Source Developer Christian Stark". The built-in updater installs only updates signed with this name.

Check it yourself

You do not have to trust StarkLLM's own screens. Turn "Fully offline" on, use the app, and look.

  1. Resource MonitorWin + R, "resmon", Network tab. With fully offline on, StarkLLM.exe, python.exe and ollama.exe should talk to 127.0.0.1 only.
  2. PowerShellList every open connection that does not go to this PC, with the program that made it:
    Get-NetTCPConnection -State Established |
  Where-Object { $_.RemoteAddress -notin '127.0.0.1','::1' } |
  ForEach-Object { $p = Get-Process -Id $_.OwningProcess -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue
    [pscustomobject]@{ Process = $p.ProcessName; Remote = "$($_.RemoteAddress):$($_.RemotePort)" } } |
  Sort-Object Process, Remote | Format-Table -AutoSize
  3. WiresharkThe strongest check: capture on your network adapter and filter out local traffic. Then turn web search on and watch DuckDuckGo appear, so you know the check works.

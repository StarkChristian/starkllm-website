Three promises, kept in the code.

01 · Local

It runs on your PC.

Documents, chats, the search index and the AI models stay on your own disk and your own graphics card. No account on a server, no telemetry.

02 · With sources

Every answer shows its source.

Answers about your documents name the file and the page. One click opens the page with the passage marked, so you can check before you act.

03 · Asks first

It asks before it acts.

Web tasks and the terminal show each step first and wait for your yes before anything that buys, sends, deletes or changes a file.

What it does for you.

Nine of the things StarkLLM does, every one of them on your PC unless it says otherwise.

Inbox

Letters, read and filed

Bills, reminders, tax notices and contracts in six languages: sender, amounts, accounts and deadlines, each found word for word on a page. One case per sender, the nearest deadline first.

Sources

Sources you can open

Click a source under an answer and the document opens at that page, with the passage marked.

Safety

Warnings for fake letters

A red box when a letter wants payment by voucher, through a link or to a new account, or hides a note to AI assistants.

Books

Whole books

Large documents get chapter summaries in the background, so "How does it end?" gets an answer, with the pages.

Images

Photos, scans and screenshots

Text in photos and scans is read on your PC. Paste a screenshot and a vision model on your PC answers.

Recordings

Meetings as documents

Recordings are written down with times on your PC. Then you ask about them like about any document.

Web tasks

Web tasks you can watch

StarkLLM works on websites in its own browser window. You see every step; sign-ins and payments stay yours.

Terminal

Help with code

In one project folder you choose: every change shown first, every command after your yes, and Undo.

Models

Two models, side by side

Ask a second local model the same question and keep the better answer.

How privacy works in StarkLLM

Android · preview

Your phone asks. Your PC answers.

The StarkLLM app for Android sends your questions and photos of letters to your PC and shows the answers with their sources. The documents and the AI model stay on the PC. The Android app is on Google Play in a test phase. If you would like it from Google Play, send your e-mail address to info@starkllm.com, and you will receive the link to Google Play.

At home, directly

Over your home network, encrypted, to the PC whose certificate the phone checked at pairing.

Away from home, through the relay

Only for a phone you allowed. Encrypted from the phone to your PC: the relay passes on bytes it cannot read.

Questions, answered.

Is StarkLLM free?

Yes. StarkLLM is free and open source under the Apache 2.0 license, for personal, research and commercial use. There is no account, no subscription and no paid tier.

Does anything leave my PC?

Not unless you switch it on. Web search, web tasks, cloud models, imports from the web, market prices, the phone relay and the update check are off until you turn them on or start them. Settings › Privacy & network lists every connection, and "Fully offline" refuses all of them.

What does my PC need?

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), 16 GB of RAM (8 GB at least), 15 GB of free disk space and Ollama, which StarkLLM can install for you. An NVIDIA graphics card with 8 GB of video memory makes answers much faster; integrated AMD Radeon and Intel Arc or Iris graphics are used too.

Do I need Docker?

No. Since version 2.2 StarkLLM brings its own engine inside the installer. Docker Desktop is an option for advanced users, nothing more.

Is there a phone app?

Yes, for Android, as a preview. It works together with your PC: the documents and the AI model stay on the PC. There is no iPhone app. The Android app is on Google Play in a test phase. If you would like it from Google Play, send your e-mail address to info@starkllm.com, and you will receive the link to Google Play.

Can I trust the answers?

Check them. The AI models are small local models and can be wrong. That is why every answer about your documents names its source, and a quotation that is not in your documents is flagged under the answer.

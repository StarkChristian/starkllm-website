Android · preview

Your phone asks. Your PC answers.

The StarkLLM app for Android works together with your PC. The documents, the index and the AI model stay on the PC; the phone asks, sends photos of letters and shows your deadlines.

What the phone does

Ask about your documents

Workspaces and chats of your PC, answers that stream in with the documents they used as chips, and the warning for quotations that are not in your documents.

Send letters

Take a photo of a letter or share a PDF from another app. Your PC reads it, also the text in photos. Photos are deleted on the phone after sending.

Deadlines

Overdue, today, this week, later. "Done" marks a deadline on the PC; a home-screen widget shows the next three.

Dictation

Hold the microphone button and speak. The speech model on your PC writes it down, not an online service.

This PC

Connection, version, AI model, backup and protection at a glance. With the right level: back up now or go fully offline, confirmed with your fingerprint.

App lock

Fingerprint when the app opens. While it is on, no screenshots and no preview in recent apps.

Paired in three steps, at home

  1. Turn it onOn the PC, open Settings › Phone and turn the phone link on. It is off by default.
  2. Show the QR codeThe code is valid for 5 minutes and works once.
  3. Scan it with the appThe phone remembers the PC's certificate and from then on talks only to that PC.

Pairing works at home only, never through the relay. Several PCs can be paired, and Settings › Phone on the PC lists every phone with "Remove".

StarkLLM

How it connects

Paired by QR code
Settings › Phone shows a QR code. A phone reaches your PC only after it was paired, and it stays paired until you remove it, or until its account signs out or changes its password. Pairing works at home only, never through the relay.
At home: direct and encrypted
At home the phone connects over your home network. The connection is encrypted, and the phone accepts only your PC's own certificate, fixed at pairing, so no other device in the Wi-Fi can pose as your PC.
Away from home: only if you allow it
Per phone, Settings › Phone › "Also away from home (through the relay)" lets it reach your PC from any network, through the StarkLLM relay (relay.starkllm.com). It is off by default. The phone then shows "Connected · away from home".
What the relay sees
The encryption runs from the phone to your PC with the same certificate as at home, so the relay passes on encrypted bytes only: no question, answer, document, device token or account. It stores and logs no content. It sees which PC a connection is for, when, and how much data flows, and it keeps a random ID and a hash of a secret for each PC.
No web search, no cloud models
A question from the phone is answered on your PC: no web search and no cloud models, also away from home. "Fully offline" on the PC closes the link and the connection to the relay.
Several accounts on one PC
Each phone belongs to the StarkLLM account that showed its QR code.

Per phone, you choose what it may do

1 / 3

Ask only

Reads workspaces, chats, the inbox and deadlines, and asks questions. It does not send documents or change deadlines.

2 / 3

Ask and send documents

Also sends photos and documents, deletes chats and marks deadlines as done.

3 / 3

Ask, send and manage this PC

Also starts a backup and can switch StarkLLM fully offline. The phone asks for your fingerprint first.

The Android app is a preview. Away from home, all data passes through the relay, encrypted; a direct connection between phone and PC is not part of this version. Away from home needs the PC on and StarkLLM running. The relay allows up to 16 phone connections at the same time and 200 MB per minute for each PC, and closes a connection after 6 minutes without any traffic.

What the relay sees, and what it does not

Away from home, the phone and your PC meet at the StarkLLM relay. The encryption runs from the phone to your PC, so the relay only passes on bytes.

The relay sees

  • Which PC a connection is for (a random ID)
  • When a connection is made
  • How much data flows

The relay never sees

  • Your questions and answers
  • Your documents and photos
  • The device token or your account

The relay keeps

  • A random ID per PC
  • A hash of the PC's secret
  • No content and no content logs

Good to know

  • The app is a preview. There is no iPhone app.
  • Away from home works only while the PC is on, StarkLLM is running and it is not fully offline.
  • Away from home, all data passes through the relay, encrypted. A direct connection between phone and PC is planned, with the relay as fallback.
  • A question from the phone is answered on your PC without web search and without cloud models, also away from home.
  • Android 10 or newer. The app has no analytics, no crash reporting service and no Google Play services.

Getting the Android app

The Android app is on Google Play in a test phase. If you would like it from Google Play, send your e-mail address to info@starkllm.com, and you will receive the link to Google Play.
